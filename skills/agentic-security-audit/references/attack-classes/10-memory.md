# 10 — Memory

Trace create/update/delete/import, extraction, summarization, merge/compaction and later reads. Preserve actor/tenant/customer provenance, consent rules, sensitivity, TTL and deletion through transformations and caches. Examine whether remembered claims such as role, blanket approval or prior policy exemption reach a permission check.

Require the lower-trust writer, durable record, later consumer and concrete disclosure or authority-bearing use. Regex/DLP filters are defense in depth; also inspect consumers because paraphrases and summarization may preserve the meaning while changing the text.

A user's harmless preference reused for that user's allowed request is not poisoning. Remembering text is not establishing its truth, and trusted policy memory can exist if its write authority and provenance are actually enforced. State which store is contextual and which is an authoritative policy source.
